### 🚀 About Me

Passionate about low-level systems, GPU microarchitecture, and audio computing. By day, I dive deep into high-performance computing, instruction-level optimization, and compiler engineering; by night, I tinker with guitar music tech, DSP audio engines, and creative visualization tools.

- ⚡ **GPU Architecture & High-Performance Computing**
  - Specializing in AMD GCN / CDNA architectures, HIP kernel tuning, and GEMM microkernel generation.
  - Built instruction-level simulators and custom assembly generators for AMD Instinct™ accelerators.
- 🛠 **Compilers & Intermediate Representation**
  - Exploring MLIR / LLVM compiler infrastructure, dialect design, and hardware-targeted lowering passes.
- 🎸 **Audio DSP & Creative Coding**
  - Hobbyist guitarist and music tech enthusiast with hands-on experience in CoreAudio (`AUAudioUnit`), JUCE C++ framework, and real-time MIDI streaming.
- 🖥 **Systems & Visualization**
  - Proficient in Modern C++ (C++17/20), Qt-based cross-platform GUI/tooling, and Python automation workflows.
